# Brief: Core, an introductory film (9:16)

- **What it's for:** Core by Ainfinite AI is an enterprise "intelligence layer" that connects company knowledge, systems and AI models through one governed foundation, and powers assistants, agents, search, workflows and AI products on top of it.
- **Who's watching:** CIOs, CTOs, heads of data and AI, and digital-transformation leads at mid-size and large organisations, including regulated ones: banking, government, defence, healthcare and critical infrastructure. They scroll LinkedIn, Instagram and Shorts on a phone. They care about three things: their data is scattered; they want AI that answers from *approved* sources; and they must keep control (permissions, private deployment). They are sceptical of hype and respond to clarity, structure and control.
- **What kind of video:** a brand introduction and explainer ("meet Core"). It is calm and confident, not hype. It moves from problem to product to how it works, then trust, deployment and a pilot CTA.
- **What they should do at the end:** *Pilot with your data* (the client's own CTA).
- **Length and size:** 27s, 1080×1920 (9:16), 60fps.
- **Brand:** background `#F7F8FB`, ink `#1A0066`, accent purple `#6100A8`, soft lavender `#F1EAFB`. Logo ring gradient: indigo `#4B28F7`, blue `#438DF9`, cyan `#6FBCFB`, lavender `#D2ADFA`, violet `#7B46FA`. Type: Manrope (geometric grotesk, tight tracking), with JetBrains Mono uppercase for small labels. The look is light, airy and clean, with thin lavender lines and white rounded cards.
- **Facts file:** `FACTS.md`. It is the only source for any word on screen.
- **Assets:** 9 screenshots of the client's post. There are no logo files, so the wordmark is rebuilt in SVG from the screenshot and a human should swap in the official file. There is no footage.
- **Style references:** none were supplied, and the reference site was unreachable from this sandbox. I used the kit's motion notes (`kit/references/launch-film-notes.md` and `motion-grammar.md`): the foreground fly-through, the logo as a portal, a wall collapsing to one actor, a continuous canvas, exploded layers, and selection expanding into the next scene.
- **Audio:** the network blocked music libraries. The score is original and composed in code (120 BPM; cuts land on the 0.5s grid). Effects come from the library bundled with HyperFrames, softened per `kit/references/audio.md`. The film must work with the sound off.
- **Never claim:** any number, customer, rating, outcome, certification or price.
