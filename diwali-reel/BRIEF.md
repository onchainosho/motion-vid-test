# Diwali greeting reel: Softlabs Group & Ainfinite AI

- **Purpose:** a festive Diwali greeting from Softlabs Group and Ainfinite AI that also showcases their two products, CORE and ADAM.
- **Viewers:** clients, partners, employees and followers on Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp status, mostly on phones. They want something warm, short and shareable, not a product pitch.
- **Action at the end:** feel the greeting and remember the brands (no hard CTA).
- **Length and size:** 10s, 1080×1920 (9:16), 60fps.
- **Brand:**
  - Logo files supplied for CORE (navy and blue-violet ring), ADAM (navy and cyan) and Softlabs Group (red and navy). No Ainfinite AI logo was supplied, so it is set as a text wordmark in Manrope; swap in the real logo when available.
  - Diwali look: a deep indigo night sky, warm gold sparks and diya light, then a warm ivory page so the logos keep their true colours.
  - Type: Playfair Display for the greeting and Manrope for small text.
- **Copy (facts file):** only the client's lines, verbatim.
  - "A spark of an idea."
  - "A world of possibilities."
  - "This Diwali, may your brightest ideas come to life."
  - "Happy Diwali" (the 🪔 rendered as a drawn diya)
  - "From all of us at"
  - "Softlabs Group & Ainfinite AI"
- **Sequence (client):** 0–2s rockets rise with "A spark of an idea."; 2–5s bursts reveal CORE and ADAM; 5–9s greeting with both company logos. Extended to 10s so the greeting can be read (about 4.5s).
- **Audio:** original warm festive score composed in code (drone, plucked melody and bells; 120 BPM, so the cuts at 2.0 and 5.0 land on beats). Effects come from the bundled HyperFrames library (whoosh for the rockets, pop and sparkle for the bursts) and are kept under the music. Music libraries are blocked in this sandbox.
- **Never:** invented claims, other logos, or recoloured brand logos.
