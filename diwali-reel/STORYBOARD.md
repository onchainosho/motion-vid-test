# Storyboard: 10.0s, 1080×1920, 60fps, 120 BPM (cuts at 2.0 and 5.0 land on beats)

Carried object: **the light**. Rocket sparks become the bursts, the bursts' glow floods the sky into warm ivory, the logos are lit by a sweep of that light, and a single diya flame carries into the greeting.

| # | Time | On screen | Why | Exit / carry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–1.9 | **Frame 0 is finished:** deep indigo night sky with faint stars, a row of lit diyas along the bottom edge, and two rockets already climbing with golden spark trails. **"A spark of an idea."** is set in gold italic serif at the top, already readable | The idea starts as a spark | Rocket 1 slows at its apex (power2.out); the line lifts and fades |
| 2 | 1.9–2.6 | Rocket 1 bursts at (360, 760), then rocket 2 at (720, 640) 0.25s later: golden peonies with white-hot cores, trails, gravity and twinkle. Fills about 80% of the frame | The big moment | The bursts' glow keeps expanding |
| 3 | 2.6–3.4 | The glow floods outward from both burst centres and turns the night into warm ivory (radial reveal). Sparks drift down as gold glitter | Light fills the world; the logos need a light ground for their true colours | Glitter keeps falling |
| 4 | 3.0–5.0 | CORE (760px wide) and ADAM (700px wide) rise into place where the bursts were, each lit by a gold sweep of light. **"A world of possibilities."** is set in navy serif at the top. A thin gold divider sits between the logos | Product reveal | The logos lift and fade upward; one spark falls to become the diya flame |
| 5 | 5.0–10.0 | Greeting: a faint gold rangoli ring rotates slowly behind. **"This Diwali, may your brightest ideas come to life."** (with "brightest ideas" in gold italic), large gold **"Happy Diwali"** with a drawn diya whose flame flickers, then **"From all of us at"** and the footer row [Softlabs Group logo] **&** [Ainfinite AI]. Glitter drifts and the frame pushes in slowly | The greeting and the senders | The final hold is about 2.5s with a gentle push |

Signature moments:
1. Two sparks climb in the dark and open into golden bursts that light the whole sky.
2. The light from the bursts washes the night into warm ivory, and the products appear in that light.
3. A falling spark lands as the flame of the diya next to "Happy Diwali".
