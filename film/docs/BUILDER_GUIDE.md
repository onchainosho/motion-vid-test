# Builder guide (read fully before writing code)

Project: `/home/user/motion-vid-test/film`. A 30 s, 1080×1920, 60 fps Instagram Reel, built as one HTML page animated on one paused GSAP timeline (`index.html` → `js/main.js`). Read these first: `docs/BRIEF.md`, `docs/FACTS.md`, `docs/STORYBOARD.md`, then `js/core.js`, `js/main.js`, `css/base.css`, and the two finished reference scenes `js/scenes/cover.js` and `js/scenes/t1.js`. Copy their idioms.

## Hard rules
1. **Edit only your own scene files** (`js/scenes/<id>.js`). Never edit `core.js`, `main.js`, `base.css` or `index.html`. If you need a helper, define it inside your scene file. Lab pages you create (`lab-<name>.html` in the film root) are fine.
2. **Determinism.** Every value is a function of timeline time. No `Date.now`, `setTimeout`, `requestAnimationFrame` or unseeded `Math.random` (use `rng(seed)` from core). Canvas/3D drawing goes in `onFrame(t => ...)`, which receives the master time `t` in seconds.
3. **Absolute times.** Scenes add tweens to the master timeline `tl` at absolute times (seconds), inside their window from `SCENES` in core (`s`, `e` are passed in). The layer becomes visible at `s - meta.pre` (default 0.4) and hidden at `e + meta.post` (default 0.4). Everything must be off-screen or invisible by `e + post`.
4. **Words.** Only words in `docs/FACTS.md`. At most 2 handwritten labels per tip. No invented numbers.
5. **Safe zone.** All key content stays inside x 64–1000 and y 200–1540. Visuals live in about y 700–1520. Nothing may overlap the header (y 150–180) or the headline/box block.
6. **Text block (tips 1–8).** Call `tipHeadline(ctx, [line1, line2])`. It places the headline at y=220 and handles the in/out from opposite sides. The orange answer box is the persistent `#box`. Its geometry is owned by `main.js` and morphs automatically at each scene boundary (the morph runs from `s-0.22` to `s+0.2`; the new text fades in from `s+0.08`). Declare the answer with `export const meta = { box: { lines: ['...'] } }`; width is fitted automatically, at x=64 and y=472, with height 132 for 1 line and 214 for 2 lines. The box bottom is therefore y=604 or y=686. You may animate transforms on `ctx.box` (y, scale, rotation) for a moment, but return them to 0/1 before `e - 0.25`.
7. **Motion grammar.** One main movement leads; smaller ones layer under it; they overlap. Things land slowly (`E.land` / expo.out) and leave fast (`E.fast` / expo.in). No linear motion except scans and playheads. Nothing in the frame is still for more than ~0.4 s. **Every action shows a result** (a scan → ticks; a drop → impact; a click → a new state). Put key events on beats: multiples of 0.25 s, preferably 0.5 s.
8. **Look.** The brand is paper, ink, orange `#EC6327`, taped polaroids, torn paper tags, hand-drawn orange arrows, and black-outlined orange boxes. Use the helpers in core: `polaroid`, `tag`, `hand`, `tape`, `svg/path/arrow/tick`, `prepDraw`. Photos live in `assets/photos/*.jpg` (list the folder). Lead visuals fill most of the visual zone, so no small cards floating in empty space. Use soft drop shadows as in `.polaroid`.
9. **Handoffs** (`HANDOFF` in core): `t1t2` is the desert polaroid at 5.0 s (`final-desert`, outer geometry x96 y760 w470 h360, rot −5, padding 16, `.polaroid` with `.ph` and `img`). Tip 2 must show an identical polaroid at exactly those pixels from 5.0 s (hidden before 5.0) and then animate it. `t7t8` is the TEST 01 frame at 20.0 s. Tip 7 ends with its TEST 01 photo element at outer x352 y742 w376 h668 (rot 0, a 9:16 screen, the `test01` photo with object-fit cover). Tip 8 shows its phone screen at exactly those pixels at 20.0 s.

## How to check your work (do it, don't skip)
- **Stills:** `node lab/stills.mjs index.html /tmp/<you>/st 5.0 5.5 ...`, then look at the PNGs. It prints page errors, which you must fix.
- **Motion proof clip:** `lab/mk.sh <name> <start> <end>` creates `lab-<name>.html`. Render it with `npx hyperframes render . -c lab-<name>.html -o renders/lab-<name>.mp4 --quiet` from the film dir (about 15 s of wall time per second of video). Make a dense contact sheet: `ffmpeg -i renders/lab-<name>.mp4 -vf "fps=10,scale=270:-1,tile=6x5" /tmp/<you>/sheet.png`.
- Check: no text collisions, nothing clipped by mistake, no pops (look for one-frame jumps), and nothing frozen.
- Don't judge quality yourself beyond fixing obvious bugs. An independent critic will review. Report back with the files changed, the lab clip path, and any known weak spots.

## v2 changes (after full-film critic F1, docs/critic/f1-full.md)
- **Bigger type:** tip headlines are now up to 176 px (was 132), fitted to 952 px wide. The box moved to **y=544**, so its bottom is **y=676** (1 line) or **y=758** (2 lines). Visuals start about 30 px below the box and may run to y 1560.
- **The box is never empty:** the old answer rolls up out of the box while the new one rolls in (main.js). Nothing for you to do.
- **Safe zone (stricter):** below y≈1000, key content (text, faces, the main subject) stays at **x ≤ 940**, because the Reels right-side buttons sit there. Decorative bleed past the edge is fine. Nothing key below y 1560.
- **Exits never pass through text:** never send a visual up through the headline, box or header. Exit down, sideways, or scale away in place.
- **No near-still windows:** the paper drift does not count. Every 0.3 s something meaningful moves; a settled hold needs a 3–4% push or secondary motion.
- **Contrast:** any settled text must be ≥4.5:1 against what is behind it. Orange text on paper fails (3.1:1); use ink, or the darker orange `#C24E17` (4.5:1), for text that must be read.
- **Vary scale:** each scene should feel like its own composition (close-up / wide / full-bleed / type-impact), not "the same layout with a different picture".
