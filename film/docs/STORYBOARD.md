## Three signature moments (no effect names)
1. **The blurry draft becomes the final shot.** The same desert photo goes from chunky draft to crisp final render once the three checks tick.
2. **The grey toy street becomes the real street.** The block car on its orange path turns into the photographed car in the same place in frame.
3. **Every answer piles up into one checklist.** The orange answer box that carried every tip becomes "CHECK THESE FIRST.", the 8 rows tick off on the beat, then it grows into "FOLLOW POSSIBLE LABS.".

## Sound off
Every tip's headline plus orange answer is the complete instruction. The visual shows the action and its result. The CTA is ink-on-paper text for 4 s.
